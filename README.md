# 🏦 Bank Customer Churn Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes bank customer churn using Excel,
MySQL, and Power BI to identify customer segments
associated with higher churn and provide actionable
business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Excel
- MySQL
- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Analyze churn by geography
- Analyze churn by number of products
- Compare active and inactive customers
- Analyze customer behavior
- Provide business recommendations

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Overall customer KPIs and churn performance.

### 2. Customer Risk Analysis
Identifies customer characteristics associated with higher churn.

### 3. Customer Behavior
Compares churned and retained customers.

### 4. Geography & Product Analysis
Analyzes churn across geography and product usage.

### 5. Customer Details
Provides customer-level filtering and analysis.

### 6. Key Insights & Recommendations
Summarizes findings and business recommendations.

## 📈 Key KPIs

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- Average Balance
- Average Credit Score
